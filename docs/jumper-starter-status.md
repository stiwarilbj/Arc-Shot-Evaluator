## Publication gate

complete release still needs 300 shots across 60 players; an omitted designation
uses the complete requirement. Neither designation relaxes identity, provenance,
full-motion review, masking, timeline, checksum, deduplication, or size checks.

The next acquisition pass must supply at least two usable full shooting motions
before offline mask correction and pair review can establish a publishable starter.
